# The Globe Diary

A global interactive 3D diary and emotional connection app where people record reflections on a world map and discover they are never alone.

## The Unique Kernel
The feeling of immediate, global solidarity: opening a living world globe to see how people in different corners of the Earth are navigating their days and mental health, with the ability to share your own reflection, browse a personal and collective timeline, and send silent, heartfelt emotional support to pins across the world.

## Who It's For
A person navigating daily challenges, feelings of isolation, or mental health conditions (such as schizophrenia, anxiety, or depression) who journals to stay grounded, but wants the comforting realization that others out there understand, are finding stability, and are walking beside them.

## The Core Loop
1. **Explore the Globe:** Spin an interactive 3D globe to see glowing mood pins across continents.
2. **Read & Connect:** Tap a pin to read an authentic personal reflection and send an emotional reaction ("You're not alone", "Warmth", "Strength").
3. **Reflect & Drop a Pin:** Check in with your current feeling rating and a short reflection, adding your story to the globe.
4. **Browse the Timeline:** Switch to a chronological timeline of entries to track reflections over time, connecting time and space.
5. **Gentle Exit Check-In:** A compassionate prompt ensuring the user feels heard and cared for whenever they wrap up.

## Inspiration & Identity
- **Tone:** Calming, compassionate, grounding, and hopeful. A deep midnight dark mode with glowing warm pin lights, making the world feel like a constellation of shared human experience.
- **Rooted in:** The author's personal journey of daily paper journaling, navigating schizophrenia toward stability, and a desire to make the world a warmer, more connected place.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
"I was recording my diaries and memories daily and how I feel on a piece of paper and also I'm diagnosed with a disease called schizophrenia and want to help other people with the same disease out there on how my life is stable right now with it and help make the world a better place to live... that they are not alone in this world."

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a 60-second screen demo:
1. The app loads with a glowing 3D interactive globe with pre-seeded reflections across multiple countries.
2. The user spins the globe, clicks a pin in another country, reads an uplifting reflection, and sends an emotional reaction with one click.
3. The user opens the "Timeline" view to see reflections arranged chronologically with mood indicators.
4. The user clicks "Add Diary Entry", inputs their mood rating and a short note, and watches a new glowing pin immediately appear at their chosen location on the globe.
5. When triggering the check-in / close action, a gentle modal pops up: *"Remember, you are not alone in this world. How are you feeling right now?"*

## The POC Boundary
- **3D Interactive Globe:** Smooth rotation, zoom, country/city pin markers with mood colors.
- **Pin Diary Modal:** Reading reflections, author's chosen name/country, mood score, and date.
- **Emotion Reactions:** Quick-reaction buttons on entries (e.g., 💙 "You're Not Alone", 🫂 "Sending Warmth", ✨ "Strength").
- **Diary Creation:** Simple form to write an entry, pick a location and mood rating (1–5 or mood descriptor), and pin it live.
- **Timeline View:** Chronological list/feed of entries with timestamps, connected to globe pins.
- **Compassionate Check-In:** Caring exit/check-in prompt.
- **Sample Data:** Seeded realistic reflections from diverse global locations so the world is immediately alive.

## Later
- Bulk file/handwritten diary text import with automatic timestamp/event parsing.
- Private "boost" to verified counselors, doctors, or community admins.
- Community matching (connecting users with similar diary themes or shared conditions).
- External social sharing links (WhatsApp, Telegram, X, Facebook).
- Virtual currency and reward points for profile badges and themes.

## Explicitly Cut
- **Real-money payments & subscriptions:** Excluded to keep the POC 100% focused on emotional impact and community connection rather than payment gateway complexity.
- **Calendar appointment scheduling:** Excluded to keep the scope tightly focused on reflection and solidarity rather than clinic management.
